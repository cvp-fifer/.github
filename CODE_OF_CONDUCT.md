# Code of Conduct

This applies org-wide to every repository under
[`cvp-fifer`](https://github.com/cvp-fifer).

We're a small, professional team building federal software. The bar is
simple: **be respectful, be constructive, and assume good faith.**

- Critique ideas and code, not people. Keep feedback specific and
  actionable.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Keep discussion on-topic and free of internal, customer, or agency
  identifiers that don't belong in a code repository.

## Reporting

If someone's conduct is a problem, contact a maintainer directly. For
anything sensitive, use the private channel described in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) rather than a public issue. Maintainers will
handle reports discreetly and decide on any response, up to removing a
contributor's access.
